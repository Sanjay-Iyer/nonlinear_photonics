## Pass condition

E_n(k), state character, HH/LH mixing and the optical matrix elements are compared across the in-plane angles. PASS is reached when the maximum angular spread is quantified against an explicit tolerance and the recommendation for 27L is stated. If the spread is negligible, the correct outcome is to record 27L as NOT NEEDED - that is a successful result, not a failure.

## The controlled change

The in-plane direction of the dispersion path, and nothing else. Growth is along [100] throughout, so [010] and [001] are symmetry-equivalent and the physically distinct sweep is from [010] to [011].

Fields this sub-demo may change: `direction_name`, `direction`.
At most **2** per deck. Everything else is inherited verbatim from the Demo 23 baseline, and `framework/registry.resolve` raises rather than generating a deck that breaks either rule.

| Deck | Changed fields | Question |
|---|---|---|
| `dir_yz15` | `direction`, `direction_name` | 15 degrees off [010] - where does the anisotropy first appear? |
| `dir_yz30` | `direction`, `direction_name` | 30 degrees off [010] |
| `dir_yz60` | `direction`, `direction_name` | 60 degrees off [010] - past the [011] diagonal Demo 23 already has |

## The question

Every chi2 calculation in this project integrates radially, assuming the in-plane
response is isotropic. Growth is along [100], so the in-plane plane is (100) and
[010] and [001] are symmetry-equivalent; the physically distinct sweep is from
[010] towards the [011] diagonal.

## The arms

| Direction | Angle from [010] | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| [010] | 0 deg | **reused** — `demo_results/demo23/raw/production_y_n301_k0100` |
| yz15 | 15 deg | new |
| yz30 | 30 deg | new |
| [011] | 45 deg | **reused** — `demo_results/demo23/raw/isotropy_yz45_n301_k0100` |
| yz60 | 60 deg | new |

Demo 23 already validated isotropy at 45 degrees against a 1 meV tolerance
(`validation.isotropy_absolute_tolerance_meV`). 27H fills in the angles between
and past it, and extends the comparison from energies alone to state character,
HH/LH mixing and the optical matrix elements.

## Why the tier came down

Planned as Tier 3 on the assumption every direction had to be computed. Two of
them already exist, so only three decks are new solver work — roughly 30-90
minutes rather than several hours. `config.yaml` records both
(`tier: 2`, `tier_planned: 3`) with the reason.

## A negative result is a success here

If the angular spread is below tolerance, the correct outcome is to record
**27L as NOT NEEDED**:

```bash
python run_demo27.py --demo 27L --record "NOT NEEDED" --conclusion "27H spread below tolerance"
```

That closes the most expensive calculation in the campaign — 8-24 hours — for the
price of three short runs, and it simultaneously validates every radial
calculation this project has done. Do not treat it as a failure to find
something.

## Do not do 2D here

27H computes several 1D paths. Full 2D integration is 27L, and running it
speculatively is exactly what the campaign structure exists to prevent.

## Data harvest

Whenever this sub-demo's decks are solved, the shared broad harvest in `demo27_config.yaml` is requested — eigenenergies, envelopes, kp8 spinors, CB/HH/LH/SO character, probabilities, transition energies, dipole and momentum matrix elements, oscillator strengths, band edges, alloy composition, k vectors, dispersions and masses, plus electrostatic potential and strain where the deck already computes them.

No expensive new physics is enabled merely because an output would be interesting. The harvest costs disk, not solver time.
