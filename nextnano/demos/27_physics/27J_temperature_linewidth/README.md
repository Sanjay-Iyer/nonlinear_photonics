## Pass condition

Two things are reported separately. (a) The measured band-gap and state-energy shift per kelvin, converted into a wavelength shift per feature using Demo 24's measured dLambda/dE, so the temperature effect is compared against the actual paper-model gaps rather than asserted. (b) A linewidth study in which every Gamma model is PHYSICALLY MOTIVATED - state-dependent, energy-dependent or k-dependent from a stated mechanism. Demo 26 already found that no single Gamma repairs all features (its hypothesis 8, "NOT SUFFICIENT", high confidence), so using Gamma as a free fitting parameter is out of scope and a fitted Gamma is not an acceptable conclusion here.

## The controlled change

The lattice temperature, which drives nextnano's temperature-dependent band parameters. Nothing else moves, so any energy shift is attributable to the material model rather than to the structure.

Fields this sub-demo may change: `temperature_K`.
At most **1** per deck. Everything else is inherited verbatim from the Demo 23 baseline, and `framework/registry.resolve` raises rather than generating a deck that breaks either rule.

## Two halves, and only one of them needs the solver

**(a) Temperature.** Two decks at 77 K and 200 K against the reused 300 K arm.
nextnano's temperature-dependent band parameters move the gap and the state
energies; Demo 24's measured `dLambda/dE` per feature converts that shift into a
predicted wavelength shift, so the temperature effect is compared against the
actual paper-model gaps rather than asserted.

**(b) Linewidth.** Pure post-processing on data that already exists. No solver
cost at all.

## Gamma is not a fitting parameter here

Demo 26 already swept the broadening and found that **no tested Gamma repairs all
features** — its hypothesis 8, "NOT SUFFICIENT", high confidence. The remaining
question is not "what Gamma fits" but "does a *physically motivated* Gamma model
change the line shape in the right direction". Candidates:

- state-dependent Gamma, from the differing scattering environments of states in
  the thick and thin wells
- energy-dependent Gamma, from LO-phonon emission opening above the phonon energy
- k-dependent Gamma, from the k dependence of the scattering rate

Each must come with its stated mechanism. **A fitted Gamma is not an acceptable
conclusion for this sub-demo**, and a result that says "no physically motivated
Gamma helps" is a pass.

## Scope note

The measurement in the paper is at room temperature. 27J is not proposing that
the experiment was cold; it is measuring the *sensitivity* of the states and the
gap to the material model's temperature dependence, which bounds how much of the
residual could come from parameter choices. That is why 77 K and 200 K are used
as a slope rather than as a claimed condition.

## Data harvest

Whenever this sub-demo's decks are solved, the shared broad harvest in `demo27_config.yaml` is requested — eigenenergies, envelopes, kp8 spinors, CB/HH/LH/SO character, probabilities, transition energies, dipole and momentum matrix elements, oscillator strengths, band edges, alloy composition, k vectors, dispersions and masses, plus electrostatic potential and strain where the deck already computes them.

No expensive new physics is enabled merely because an output would be interesting. The harvest costs disk, not solver time.
