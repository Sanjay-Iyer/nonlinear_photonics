## Pass condition

Every state above the Al0.55Ga0.45As barrier edge is classified by evidence, not by index: confinement fraction inside the wells, sensitivity to the domain size and to the boundary condition, and oscillator strength to the states Equation 2 uses. PASS means each such state is labelled REAL QUASI-BOUND (its energy and localization survive the domain change) or DIRICHLET BOX STATE (it moves with the walls). A box state may not be entered into Equation 2 under any circumstances, and 27K produces no chi2 contribution from unlabelled states.

## The controlled change

Many more states than the structure binds, solved on three different numerical domains. The state count is the sub-demo's subject; the domain is varied because that is the only way to tell a real quasi-bound resonance from a Dirichlet box level, and holding it fixed would make the question unanswerable rather than controlled.

Fields this sub-demo may change: `num_electrons`, `num_holes`, `output_state_count`, `quantum_region_padding_nm`, `period_barrier_nm`.
At most **4** per deck. Everything else is inherited verbatim from the Demo 23 baseline, and `framework/registry.resolve` raises rather than generating a deck that breaks either rule.

| Deck | Changed fields | Question |
|---|---|---|
| `continuum_pad2_outer18` | `num_electrons`, `num_holes`, `output_state_count` | What lies above the barrier edge on the Demo 23 domain? |
| `continuum_pad8_outer18` | `num_electrons`, `num_holes`, `output_state_count`, `quantum_region_padding_nm` | Which of those states move when the Dirichlet walls move? |
| `continuum_pad2_outer34` | `num_electrons`, `num_holes`, `output_state_count`, `period_barrier_nm` | Which of them move when the outer barrier gets thicker? |

**Basis:** 64 states is more than four times the Demo 23 production count of 14, and the eigenvalue cost grows faster than linearly in the state count, so each deck is taken as 1-3 h against the measured 10 min 20 s baseline. ## Why three decks and not one

A continuum claim is only meaningful if the domain is varied underneath it. One
deck cannot distinguish a real quasi-bound resonance from a Dirichlet box level,
because both appear as eigenvalues of the same matrix. The three decks differ in
padding (2 vs 8 nm) and outer barrier (18.2 vs 34.2 nm), so a state that keeps
its energy and its localization across all three is behaving like a resonance and
a state that moves with the walls is not.

## The classification rule

Every state above the Al0.55Ga0.45As barrier edge gets one of:

- **REAL QUASI-BOUND** — energy and localization survive the domain change, with
  measurable confinement inside the wells
- **DIRICHLET BOX STATE** — moves with the walls

**A box state may not be entered into Equation 2 under any circumstances**, and
this sub-demo produces no chi2 contribution from unlabelled states. Demo 24
already found that Demo 23's high-energy structure came from k truncation rather
than from real states; the same discipline applies here to the energy axis.

## The barrier edge is the reference

From Demo 23's `bandedges.dat`: CB 3.3244 eV, VB 1.1795 eV, barrier gap
2.145 eV. That is the line above which "bound" stops meaning bound.

## Data harvest

Whenever this sub-demo's decks are solved, the shared broad harvest in `demo27_config.yaml` is requested — eigenenergies, envelopes, kp8 spinors, CB/HH/LH/SO character, probabilities, transition energies, dipole and momentum matrix elements, oscillator strengths, band edges, alloy composition, k vectors, dispersions and masses, plus electrostatic potential and strain where the deck already computes them.

No expensive new physics is enabled merely because an output would be interesting. The harvest costs disk, not solver time.
