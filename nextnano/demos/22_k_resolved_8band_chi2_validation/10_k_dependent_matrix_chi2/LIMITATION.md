# Model D limitation

The nextnano++ deck requests all-k dipole and momentum outputs, signed/complex
where the solver supports them. Demo 22 will not equate envelope momentum with
the interband Bloch/Kane amplitude required by the validated χ² equation.

Model D remains incomplete until the Professional output demonstrates that the
required electron–hole optical amplitudes and intra-band position elements can
be exported or reconstructed in one consistent complex gauge. Model C
(energy-only) remains scientifically distinct and may use only frozen k=0
matrices from the exact same geometry.
