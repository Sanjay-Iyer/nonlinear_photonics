# Wavelength convention audit

Figure 2d labels the x axis **Fundamental Wavelength (nm)** and its caption describes SHG versus fundamental wavelength. The production mapping E=hc/lambda is therefore correct. The 2lambda and lambda/2 comparisons are diagnostics only and do not justify relabeling the paper. The 540/1080 and 760/1520 pairs naturally reflect one- and two-photon denominators, not an SH-output wavelength axis.
