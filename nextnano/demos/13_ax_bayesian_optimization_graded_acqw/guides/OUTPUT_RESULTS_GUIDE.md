# Demo 13 output results guide

_Generated by `guides13.py` — edit that, not this file._

What every important result number means, and how to avoid over-reading it.

**Units.** Every chi(2) number in this demo is a *relative* merit in arbitrary units (`a.u. (relative |chi2|)`). It is a lineshape and a trend with no absolute calibration. It is **never pm/V**, and values are only comparable between designs computed with the same mesh and settings.

## The four things that decide whether a design is any good

1. **Objective** — `relative_chi2_at_target_wavelength_abs`, higher is better.
2. **Detuning** — `absolute_detuning_nm` must be <= 15 nm. In v3 this is what failed 13 of 16 completed trials.
3. **Confinement** — `maximum_boundary_probability` must be <= 1e-3.
4. **State identity** — `state_tracking_confidence` must be >= 0.8.

**A high objective is not enough.** A design with the strongest response and a 20 nm detuning is *infeasible*, and the best-so-far curve will not move for it.

**Current result (experiment v3).** 23 proposals, 7 refused before the solver ran, 16 completed with no solver failures, **3 feasible**. The best feasible trial is **t0021** (asymmetry 0.387565, central barrier 0.85 nm, abrupt, relative chi2 at 1550 nm = 0.411027, detuning -13 nm). t0021 sits on the barrier **lower bound** and Stage 5 has not run, so it is the best design *found*, **not a validated optimum**.

## `absolute_detuning_nm`

**How far the peak sits from 1550 nm, ignoring direction.**

- **Units:** nm
- **Direction:** lower is better
- **How it is computed:** |peak_wavelength_nm - 1550|.
- **Caution:** THIS is the constrained quantity, bounded at 15 nm. It is what made 13 of 16 v3 trials infeasible.

## `best_objective_so_far`

**The best FEASIBLE objective seen up to this iteration.**

- **Units:** a.u. (objective units)
- **Direction:** higher is better
- **How it is computed:** Running maximum over valid completed trials.
- **Caution:** An invalid trial never improves it, however high its objective.

## `constrained_expected_improvement_proxy`

**Expected improvement weighted by the chance of being feasible.**

- **Units:** unspecified
- **Direction:** higher is better
- **How it is computed:** expected_improvement x probability_of_feasibility.
- **Caution:** NOT Ax's acquisition. Ax used a Monte-Carlo constrained acquisition that cannot be replayed from a snapshot.

## `expected_acquisition_value`

**What Ax's own acquisition scored this proposal at, historically.**

- **Units:** acquisition units (model dependent)
- **Direction:** higher is better
- **How it is computed:** Recorded on the generator run at proposal time.
- **Caution:** Empty for Sobol trials, which are quasi-random.

## `maximum_boundary_probability`

**The largest fraction of any single state leaking to the domain edge.**

- **Units:** probability
- **Direction:** lower is better
- **How it is computed:** Max over states of the probability inside the outer 5% of the domain.
- **Caution:** Distinct from total_boundary_probability, which sums over states. ~3e-5 in v3, so this constraint never bound.

## `maximum_feasible_grading_nm`

**The widest grade this design's geometry could build.**

- **Units:** nm
- **Direction:** neither
- **How it is computed:** central barrier thickness - minimum_flat_region_nm.
- **Caution:** Below a 0.90 nm barrier this falls under 0.80 nm and no grade fits.

## `mbm_iteration_number`

**Which model-based BO iteration this completed evaluation was.**

- **Units:** unspecified
- **Direction:** neither
- **How it is computed:** 1..10 over completed model-based trials, in trial order.
- **Caution:** The ledger's `iteration` column counts proposal ATTEMPTS and reached 17 in this 10-iteration study. Do not plot it as the BO iteration.

## `origin_independence_valid`

**Does the answer stay the same if the coordinate origin moves?**

- **Units:** 0 or 1
- **Direction:** 1 is required
- **How it is computed:** chi(2) recomputed after a 100 nm origin shift must agree.
- **Caution:** A binary flag. Enforced outside Ax; a Gaussian model cannot usefully represent a 0/1 outcome.

## `orthonormality_error`

**How far the computed states are from being mutually orthonormal.**

- **Units:** dimensionless
- **Direction:** lower is better
- **How it is computed:** Max deviation of the state overlap matrix from the identity.
- **Caution:** ~3e-7 throughout, four orders inside its 1e-3 bound, so it is deliberately NOT modelled by Ax.

## `peak_wavelength_nm`

**Where the response peaks.**

- **Units:** nm
- **Direction:** closer to 1550 nm is better
- **How it is computed:** Wavelength of the maximum of |chi(2)|.
- **Caution:** Shifts strongly with well asymmetry and barrier thickness.

## `probability_of_feasibility`

**Model's estimate that a point satisfies every modelled constraint.**

- **Units:** unspecified
- **Direction:** higher is better
- **How it is computed:** Product of per-constraint Gaussian tail probabilities.
- **Caution:** Assumes the constraints are independent; Ax does not. A proxy.

## `proposed_grading_fraction`

**What fraction of the available room the optimizer asked to grade.**

- **Units:** fraction of the feasible maximum in [0,1]
- **Direction:** neither
- **How it is computed:** The Ax coordinate, dimensionless, in [0.35, 1.0].
- **Caution:** NOT a length. Multiply by maximum_feasible_grading_nm to get nanometres, then apply mesh snapping.

## `realized_grading_thickness_nm`

**The grade width actually built in the deck.**

- **Units:** nm
- **Direction:** neither
- **How it is computed:** Mesh-snapped width; 0 for an abrupt design.
- **Caution:** The ONLY width any physical statement may use.

## `rejection_reason`

**Why a proposal was refused before the solver ran.**

- **Units:** text
- **Direction:** neither
- **How it is computed:** Category plus detail; all 7 v3 refusals were subresolution_grade.
- **Caution:** A refusal is not a duplicate and not a solver failure.

## `relative_chi2_at_target_wavelength_abs`

**How strong the second-order response is exactly at 1550 nm.**

- **Units:** a.u. (relative |chi2|)
- **Direction:** higher is better
- **How it is computed:** |chi(2)| from Demo 11 Eq. 2 evaluated at the target wavelength.
- **Caution:** A RELATIVE arbitrary-unit merit. Never pm/V. Only comparable between designs computed with the same mesh and settings.

## `relative_peak_chi2_abs`

**The strongest response anywhere in the scanned band.**

- **Units:** a.u. (relative |chi2|)
- **Direction:** higher is better
- **How it is computed:** Maximum |chi(2)| over the focused wavelength window.
- **Caution:** A high peak at the wrong wavelength does not help a 1550 nm device.

## `signed_detuning_nm`

**How far the peak sits from 1550 nm, with direction.**

- **Units:** nm
- **Direction:** closer to zero is better
- **How it is computed:** peak_wavelength_nm - 1550.
- **Caution:** REPORTED but never constrained. The sign tells you which way to move; the constraint uses the absolute value.

## `state_tracking_confidence`

**How sure the code is that it followed the same physical state.**

- **Units:** overlap in [0,1]
- **Direction:** higher is better
- **How it is computed:** Overlap of this trial's states with the reference trial's.
- **Caution:** Self-consistency, not proof. Least discriminating exactly where the barrier is thinnest and the states are near-degenerate.

## `state_tracking_margin`

**How much better the chosen assignment was than the runner-up.**

- **Units:** overlap difference
- **Direction:** higher is better
- **How it is computed:** Difference between the best and second-best overlap.
- **Caution:** A small margin with high confidence still means an ambiguous match.

## `trial_valid`

**Did this trial satisfy every constraint and QC check?**

- **Units:** boolean
- **Direction:** True is required
- **How it is computed:** All outcome constraints satisfied and all QC checks passed.
- **Caution:** An invalid trial keeps its metrics; it is a result, not a gap.
